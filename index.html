01 / About

Research that connects modalities.

I am a Senior Researcher at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA). My research spans multimodal learning, speech and audio, computer vision, and representation learning. I am especially interested in generative models that connect language with real-world signals.

I currently work on VibeVoice, a family of open-source voice AI models for long-form speech generation and recognition. My earlier work includes Kosmos-2, BEiT, and Conformer.

I completed my doctoral research at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences under the supervision of Prof. Qixiang Ye. I received my B.E. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2019.
